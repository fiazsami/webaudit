# Social App — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 March 2026.

## Introduction

This policy explains what we collect and why. We have tried to write it in plain
language.

## Information We Collect

### Information you give us

Your name, email address, date of birth, profile photograph, and anything you
post.

### Information we collect automatically

Device model, operating system version, IP address, and the pages you view
within the app. We use cookies and similar technologies to recognise you across
sessions.

### Information from others

If a friend uploads their address book, we may receive your contact details even
if you do not have an account with us.

## How We Use Information

To provide the service, to personalise what you see, and to select and measure
advertising. We use automated systems to analyse your content in order to
suggest connections and to target advertising.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with service providers who
process it on our behalf, with advertisers in aggregated or pseudonymised form,
and with law enforcement where we believe in good faith that disclosure is
required.

## Your Choices

You can download a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account at any
time from the settings page. Deleting your account removes your profile within
30 days, although backup copies may persist for up to 90 days.

## Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
their information.

## Changes

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you by email at least
30 days before they take effect.

## Contact

Write to privacy@social-app.invalid or to our postal address in the app.
